Share your distance

How this is measured. Straight-line distance from your address point to the nearest edge of the proposed yard (the Union Pacific Canal Street Yard outline in OpenStreetMap, the same outline the neighborhood dashboard uses). Addresses come from Cook County's Address Points file.

Schools and day cares on the map. The same lists as the neighborhood dashboard: every district, charter, private and parochial school, and every licensed day-care center (City of Chicago Children's Services Facility licenses) within a mile of the proposed yard. After-school-only centers and home day cares are not shown.

People and children. The same figures as the neighborhood dashboard: each census tract's residents and children under 18 (ACS 2020–2024), counted by the share of the tract's 2020 Census block population living within a given distance of the yard. Within 500 feet we show the children and everyone that close; out to a mile we show the children who live closer than you.

Your location. Turn off “Show my location on the map” to leave the dotted line and the ME marker off the map and your share images. The map then shows the whole site instead of your block.

Farther away? If an address isn't within 2 miles of the site, we show who lives within 500 feet of it instead, with a link to The Railyard Across the Street, which shows Amtrak's proposed building across the street from your address in 3D. That page looks your address up with an online map service, so the address leaves your device only if you follow the link.
