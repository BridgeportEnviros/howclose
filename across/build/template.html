How this works
  • Your address is located with a geocoding service. Nothing is stored by this page.
  • Your street's direction comes from U.S. Census TIGER street centerlines. The building is placed on the opposite side, parallel to your street.
  • Setback: a typical Chicago residential street is 34 ft curb to curb, with 15 ft from the curb to the inside edge of the sidewalk (66-ft right-of-way; CDOT Street and Site Plan Design Standards, 2007). The wall starts at that line, 32 ft from the street's center. Your actual street may be wider or narrower.
  • The building is drawn from Amtrak/WSP schematic drawings dated Feb 27, 2026: 1,209 ft long, up to 203 ft deep, shop roof 49 ft 8 in and penthouses 73 ft 4 in above the rails. It sits on a 13 ft 7 in wall, the height of the existing rail yard above the street in Bridgeport (USGS LiDAR, 2017).
  • Illustrative only. This is not an Amtrak rendering. Windows and doors are approximate. Wall color matches Amtrak's published renderings.
